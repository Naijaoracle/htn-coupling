# Stage 5 follow-up predeclaration

This follow-up was fixed after the Stage 5 amendment and before any expanded
acute run. Existing gate results remain unchanged and auditable.

## F1. Augmentation pressure diagnosis

Report the already-computed rebound augmentation pressure in mmHg beside the
ratio `100 * augmentation pressure / pulse pressure`. Compare inlet-only,
uniform `E x2.5`, and central-only `E x2.5` without rerunning openBF. A falling
index is not interpreted without its numerator and denominator.

## F2. Reflection detector audit

For each carotid and phenotype, overlay inlet-only, uniform, and central-only
pressure traces on common phase axes. Mark the global primary peak, first
post-primary minimum, and first subsequent local maximum selected by the
existing detector. Also list every detected local maximum and its prominence.
Visually classify whether the marked secondary maximum is the same morphological
feature across arms. Do not substitute a new detector after viewing the result.

## F3. Expanded acute panel

Select 20 engine-admissible HAALSI bodies from the fixed anonymous Stage 2
sample: ten male and ten female, ages 40–65, at evenly spaced ranks of the
within-sex BMI distribution. Blood pressure and outcome are not selection
variables. Run all four existing Pulse phenotypes on every body using the same
fixed 200 mL/min haemorrhage and 900 s horizon.

The primary endpoint is first time to a 30% fall from each run's median MAP over
seconds 0–29. Times to 20% and 40% falls and absolute MAP 65 are secondary.
Comparisons are paired within body against normotensive. Report median paired
difference, 95% percentile bootstrap interval with seed 20260817, the count of
bodies moving earlier/later, and two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank p-values with
Holm correction across the three phenotype comparisons. Missing crossings are
reported and not imputed.

This remains an exploratory stock-Pulse result: one engine, twenty bodies, and
acute modifier phenotypes without disease history.
